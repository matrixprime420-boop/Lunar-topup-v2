Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how LUNAR TOPUP ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you use our topup service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: name, email address and profile picture.
  • Game details: Player ID (UID) you enter for topups.
  • Payment details: amount, gateway and transaction ID. We never store your mobile banking PIN.

2. How We Use It

  • To process topup orders and wallet deposits.
  • To provide customer support and prevent fraud.
  • To send important service notices.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Information is shared only with payment and game service providers as required to complete your order, or when required by law.

4. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. No online service is 100% secure, so please keep your password private.

5. Refunds

Wrong Player IDs submitted by users are not refundable. Failed orders are refunded to your wallet.

6. Your Rights

You may request account deletion or data correction at any time through our support channels.

7. Contact

For questions, contact us via WhatsApp or Telegram from the support button.
